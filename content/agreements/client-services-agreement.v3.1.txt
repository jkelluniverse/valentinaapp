2. NATURE OF THE SERVICES — PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT, NOT HEALTHCARE

The services are personal-development facilitation and PSYCH-K® belief-change work provided by Valentina Vélez, a Certified PSYCH-K® Facilitator and Personal Development Consultant. The services are educational, reflective, and forward-focused. They are NOT psychotherapy, psychological services, psychiatric care, counseling, medical care, diagnosis, or treatment — and not a substitute for any of them. No therapist–patient, psychologist–patient, physician–patient, or other healthcare relationship is created.

The Practitioner holds dual Bachelor of Science degrees in Biomedical Sciences and Psychology (with a neuropsychology specialization) from the University of South Florida and is a certified PSYCH-K® Facilitator at the Master/Advanced level. She is not a licensed psychologist, therapist, or healthcare provider and does not provide psychotherapy, psychological services, or mental health treatment.

References to mindset patterns, belief systems, personal development research, or reflective frameworks are educational descriptions only. The Practice does not administer or interpret psychological tests, diagnose mental health disorders or medical conditions, or advise anyone to change or discontinue licensed care or medication.

You affirm you are not relying on the services to diagnose or treat any medical or mental-health condition, and you agree not to delay, replace, or discontinue professional care or medication based on this work without consulting the appropriate licensed provider. The Practitioner may recommend licensed care and may pause, narrow, or decline services where she believes your needs fall outside this scope or that a higher level of care is appropriate.

INTAKE SCREENING. Before beginning services, you will complete a brief intake form. If you indicate you are currently seeking treatment for, or have been diagnosed with, a mental health condition requiring clinical care, the Practitioner may refer you to a licensed mental health professional and may decline or defer services until you confirm you have appropriate licensed support in place. This practice is not a substitute for therapy, and concurrent work with a licensed provider is encouraged when clinical needs are present.

5. NOT AN EMERGENCY OR CRISIS SERVICE

THE SERVICES, THE PORTAL, AND ALL MESSAGING FEATURES ARE NOT AN EMERGENCY OR CRISIS SERVICE AND ARE NOT MONITORED CONTINUOUSLY. Messaging is asynchronous: the Practitioner responds within her own working rhythm, and stated response expectations are good-faith rhythms, not commitments. The portal's automated features may display supportive resources or bring a message to the Practitioner's attention; they are best-effort aids that can be delayed, incomplete, wrong, or unavailable, and they create no duty to monitor, detect, or intervene.

If you are in crisis or an emergency, do not use the portal to seek help: call 911, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or contact your local emergency services — and if outside the United States, the emergency resources where you are. You agree to do so. Where the Practice reasonably believes there is an imminent risk of serious harm, it may disclose limited information to emergency services or others as permitted or required by law; you understand the Practice may not have enough information to locate or assist you.

6. USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

The Practitioner uses AI tools under her oversight. AI may transcribe session audio and handwriting; organize and surface themes from your journaling, messages, and assessments; generate draft readings from your chart and assessment data; and help draft session materials and reflective questions. You consent to this processing, including by the vendors in Section 7.

Safeguards the platform enforces: nothing AI-generated becomes part of your record or is published to you without the Practitioner's explicit review and approval; AI output is labeled and treated as an interpretive draft, never a conclusion; nothing is auto-diagnosed (and per Section 2, nothing is diagnosed at all); and your corrections to your own information are honored.

AI output can still be incomplete, biased, or wrong; its accuracy is not warranted; human review reduces but does not eliminate the risk of error; and no AI output is medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or other licensed professional advice. Some portal features may be unavailable if you do not consent to the AI processing they require.

14. SESSION RECORDING

No one may record a session — including you — unless every required party has knowingly consented. Florida law (§934.03) makes it a criminal offense to record a private conversation without all parties' consent. Secret recording by any party violates this Agreement, may violate Florida criminal law, and may result in immediate termination of services.

The Practice records only under the separate Recording Addendum: a standing, versioned, revocable consent plus a per-session confirmation, enforced by the platform (no recording is processed without your stored consent on file). Florida law requires all parties' consent; if any other person is present, that person must separately consent before recording. You may revoke recording consent prospectively at any time; revocation stops future recording and does not make a prior lawful recording unlawful — treatment of existing recordings is governed by the Recording Addendum and Addendum R. You may not secretly record, copy, stream, or distribute any session.

Any court action must be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts serving Orange County, Florida, and each party consents to that jurisdiction and venue. Each party irrevocably waives any objection to venue in Orange County, Florida, including any claim that such venue is inconvenient or improper, and waives any right to transfer or remove the action to another jurisdiction.

In any action to collect unpaid fees or respond to an invalid payment dispute, the prevailing Practice is entitled to its reasonable attorney fees and costs.
